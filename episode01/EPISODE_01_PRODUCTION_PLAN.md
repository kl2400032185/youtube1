## 1. HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT — 6-STEP PIPELINE

1. **GENERATE IMAGES** — For each scene, paste the *Global Style Master* + that scene's *Image Prompt* into your image generator. Keep aspect ratio 16:9 for all scenes. Use the consistency rules (Section 8) to lock character faces.
2. **ANIMATE EACH IMAGE** — Feed each generated image into your image-to-video tool with that scene's *Image-to-Video Prompt*. Keep clips 8–15 sec to match the stated durations.
3. **RECORD VOICE-OVER** — Record the Telugu narration scene by scene exactly as written (Section B of each scene). Honour the `…` pauses. Record all dialogue lines (Section C) in the character voices defined in the Audio Master Plan.
4. **MIX MUSIC + SFX** — Place BGM themes per the BGM Master Plan timestamps, and the per-scene SFX listed in Section E of each scene. Duck music under narration.
5. **EDIT TOGETHER** — Assemble on a 13:58 timeline using the Final Timeline table. Apply each scene's stated *Transition* (Section J).
6. **THUMBNAIL + TITLE** — Generate the thumbnail art from Section 13 and overlay the Telugu text manually in an editor (AI tools garble Telugu script — see Section 13 notes).

**Name in-world:** a forest ashrama on a Himalayan riverbank, cradled between old forest and distant snow peaks. (Narration keeps the place nameless and timeless.)

> ASHRAMA-ID: Vyasa's forest ashrama on the green bank of a wide, clear Himalayan river — five round clay huts with sloping golden thatch roofs, a central teaching platform of packed earth beneath an enormous ancient banyan tree with thick aerial roots, a small open homa pavilion with a square brick fire-altar, narrow stone paths lined with tulasi pots and flowering jasmine bushes, mango and jackfruit trees, grazing cows and calm spotted deer, peacocks, a low wooden grantha-kutira (manuscript hut) with palm-leaf bundles tied in cloth stacked on shelves, a small brass temple-bell hanging from a wooden post near the banyan, worn stone steps (ghat) descending to the river, snow-touched mountains far away in the hazy blue distance.

**Layout (keep identical geography in every scene):**

- BANYAN + teaching platform at the centre of the clearing.
- 5 huts arc behind the platform; the GRANTHA-KUTIRA (manuscript hut) stands slightly apart, nearer the river.
- HOMA pavilion to the right of the platform; kitchen fire area behind the huts.
- Stone path: gate (simple wooden arch with hanging brass bell) → courtyard → platform → ghat.

**Ashrama colour moods by time of day:**
- Dawn: misty blue-grey → gold, volumetric god-rays through banyan leaves.
- Midday: clear emerald + warm earth.
- Golden hour: amber, long shadows, rim-lit leaves.
- Night: blue-silver moonlight, one warm oil-lamp accent.
