## 6. 🔍 Production & pronunciation notes

- ⚠️ **Critical:** **తినను** (*thinnanu*, long *i*, double *n*) = **"I will NOT eat"**.
  If the VA says *thinānu* (తినాను) it means "I ATE it" and the whole joke dies.
  Give this note to your voice talent/TTS explicitly.
- The aroma steam must read instantly as biryani: golden steam curl + a tiny
  visual of the handi in the background helps — no English "smell" text needed.
- Heart-eyes and floating are cartoon shorthand — keep the rest of the scene
  restrained so the gag pops.
- Quick cuts: no shot longer than 2.5s. This is the "reel pace" episode.
- If your tool struggles with hands holding food, show the eating button in a
  medium shot with the handi and full cheeks instead of close-up fingers.
