## 6. 🔍 Production & pronunciation notes

- **చెయ్యి** (*cheyyi*) = "do it" (casual imperative of చేయు) — NOT *chēyi* (hand).
- **రూటర్** = *ROO-ter*. **వైఫై** = *WY-fy* (common Telugu pronunciation).
- **అయితే?** must feel like a full stop to drama — slower than normal speech.
- The punchline works because Amma's energy **never changes**. Direct the VA: zero drama.
- Router red→green light turn is the cleanest visual "Wi-Fi is back" signal — no English UI text needed.
- If your video tool can't do Telugu VO: generate with mouthing only, lay VO in the edit (recommended anyway for control).
