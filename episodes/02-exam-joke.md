## 6. 🔍 Production & pronunciation notes

- **పాస్ చేయించు** (*pass cheyyinchu*) = "make [me] pass" — the -ఇంచు causative is the
  correct colloquial praying form; don't use చేయి (that's "do it").
- **పేపర్** here = the exam question paper — every Indian student knows the word;
  keep it (Telugu-scripted) rather than ప్రశ్నాపత్రం.
- The joke's engine is **the identical promise twice** — direct the VA to deliver
  the second one with *more* conviction, not less.
- Time card "కొన్ని నిమిషాల తర్వాత…" can be text-only (no VO) — funnier and
  saves the narrator voice; keep the SRT line either way for reading viewers.
- Keep the god picture respectful: simple framed silhouette/flowers, no depiction
  of a specific deity face — safer for platforms and generation tools.
