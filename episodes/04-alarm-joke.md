## 6. 🔍 Production & pronunciation notes

- **ఇంకో ఐదు నిమిషాలు** (*inko aidu nimishaalu*) = pure-Telugu "5 more minutes";
  if you prefer the natural Tanglish "ఇంకో 5 minutes…" it's equally authentic —
  pick one and keep subtitles consistent with the VO.
- **9 అయిపోయిందా?!** — stress **అయ్యో** (the gasp) and rush the rest.
- The phone screen must show only **digits + a bell icon** — any English word
  ("Snooze", "Alarm") violates the no-English-text rule and often garbles in AI video.
- The slap-off gag works because each attempt is lazier and faster — tell the
  animator/editor to cut quicker each repetition.
- Same చైతు (green hoodie) as episodes 2 & 5 — keep the model identical.
