## 6. 🔍 Production & pronunciation notes

- **ఛార్జర్** (*CHAAR-jar*) — the **ఛ** must be aspirated (*ch*ha), not చ.
- **పోతోంది** (*pothondhi*) = "is going/dying" — present continuous; keep the
  dying-hero slow delivery.
- Phone screens: only **"1%" + battery icon**, then **black screen** — no English
  words ("Low battery", "Charger") anywhere; AI tools garble them anyway.
- The rescue charger must enter frame **silently from a friend's hand** — the
  visual says it all; no extra dialogue needed.
- Same చైతు model as episodes 2 & 4 (green hoodie) for series continuity.
