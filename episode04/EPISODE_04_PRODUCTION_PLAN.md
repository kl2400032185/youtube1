**Palette/world:** ancient forest ashrama, river, giant banyan+tamarind trees, blue mountains, palm-leaf manuscripts, thatched hermitage huts, sacred fire, birds, deer, lotus pond, golden sunrise/sunset. Style: premium 2.5D cinematic anime, Indian mythological painting, volumetric golden light, family-friendly, NO existing studio/franchise style.
